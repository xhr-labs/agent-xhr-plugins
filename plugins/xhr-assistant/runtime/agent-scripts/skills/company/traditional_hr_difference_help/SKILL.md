### Direct answer
Unlike traditional HR platforms, which are closed and rigid, X-HR is an extensible ecosystem: innovative by design, *Self-service* at its core, *Creative* in functionality, *Reliable* in compliance and data control. 

You don’t adapt to the platform, the platform adapts to you. X-HR provides Relationship-Based Access (ReBAC), which protects employee data and defines access by team, org chart, or function. It has access and permissions structure that mimics your actual organization structure and is much more flexible than traditional RBAC
