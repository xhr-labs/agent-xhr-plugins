### Direct answer
Reward elements are reusable compensation or benefits items that help HR define what can be included in compensation packages. They act as building blocks for package groups and C&B components.

Use reward elements when the business wants consistent names and definitions for pay, benefits, allowances, or other reward items before assigning or grouping them in broader compensation structures.
