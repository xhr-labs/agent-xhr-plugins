# My Earnings Help

## Intent: payroll-my-earnings-help
### User request patterns
- view my earnings
- check my pay history
- find my gross and net pay
- view my pay period and pay date
- explain why My Earnings is unavailable

### Retrieval tags
- payroll
- my-earnings
- payslip
- pay-history
- direct-answer

### Answer objective
Explain where employees see their own payroll history and its visibility controls.

### Instructions
- Answer directly without calling executable tools.
- Clarify that My Earnings is self-service for the authenticated employee.

### Direct answer
Open **My Profile**, select **Compensation**, and find **My Earnings**.

The table shows the employee's own pay history, including pay period, pay date, gross pay, and net pay when available. Money values are masked by default and can be revealed with the visibility control; they automatically mask again after a short period.

My Earnings appears when the Payroll app and the required payroll data are available for the company.
