# Employee Change Records Help

## Intent: employment-records-employee-change-records-help

### Answer objective
Explain what employee change records capture and how they differ from profile edits.

### Instructions
- Answer directly without calling executable tools.

### Direct answer
Open [Contracts & Company Decisions]({{ccd_contracts_url}}).

Employee change records capture structured employment changes such as job updates, compensation changes, and related employment metadata. They provide a record-oriented view of changes instead of only updating the current employee profile.

This is useful when HR needs review context, approvals, attachments, and activity history around a change. Access to create, edit, or view records depends on Employment Records permissions.
