# My Assets Help

## Intent: asset-inventory-my-assets-help
### User request patterns
- show my assigned assets
- view my assets in Asset Inventory
- find details for an asset assigned to me
- acknowledge receipt of an assigned asset
- explain employee asset self service

### Retrieval tags
- asset-inventory
- my-assets
- employee-self-service
- assigned-assets
- direct-answer

### Answer objective
Explain how employees use My Assets to view assigned assets and related details.

### Instructions
- Answer directly without calling executable tools.
- Keep employee self-service separate from admin asset lifecycle management.

### Direct answer
Open **Asset Inventory** and go to **My Assets**. Employees can review assets assigned to them, open the asset detail view, and see status, asset metadata, and assignment information where permitted.

If receipt acknowledgement is enabled for the assignment, the employee can confirm that they received the asset from the My Assets flow. Admin-only lifecycle actions such as assigning, returning, or editing inventory records stay in the admin asset management area.
