### Direct answer
Open [Reports]({{attendance_reports_url}}).

Attendance reports help managers and HR review attendance activity over time. Reports can include approved or waiting-for-approval timesheet lists, employee detail views, annual report tables, progress hours, signed durations, and overtime-related values where configured.

Reports default to Approved for the current month. Select Waiting for approval to investigate pending submissions, or Annual to review approved facts and 12-month balance progression. An empty result is a UI empty state, not proof that the company has no records; check the status, period, filters, permissions, and employee detail. A negative balance represents undertime or a negative time balance. Use approval flows when the user needs to approve or reject specific submitted timesheets.
