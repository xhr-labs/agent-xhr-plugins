## How to Set Up the Gantt Chart for a Space or Project to Reflect the Roadmap

To visualize your project roadmap on the Gantt chart, follow these steps:

1. **Set Start and End Dates** for each task.  
   - You can do this directly from the **Gantt Chart** view.  
2. **Hover over a Task Line** and click to add a **timeline bar**.  
3. **Drag the Bar** to adjust the task’s **start and end dates** according to your plan.  

> 💡 **Tip:** Keeping task timelines up to date helps your Gantt chart accurately reflect your overall project progress and roadmap
