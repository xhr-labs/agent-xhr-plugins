### Direct answer
Review forms support structured stages such as **Self Assessment**, **Manager Review**, and **HR Notes**.

In the form's **Detail** tab, authorized users can enable stages, choose the assignee role or employee, and configure whether later reviewers may see completed previous stages. Questions are configured in the **Forms** tab.

When Workflow assigns a review step, the reviewer opens it from Task Hub. Only the assigned step is editable. Permitted previous steps remain visible as read-only context, and the submission is retained as part of the grouped review report.
