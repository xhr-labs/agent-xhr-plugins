### Direct answer
**Final Settlement** is the dedicated app surface for end-of-employment settlement workflows. It is intended to support HR and payroll teams when an employee exits and settlement-related work needs to be tracked separately from ordinary payroll or profile updates.

Current availability and workflow depth can depend on tenant rollout and backend readiness. Use Payroll and Employee lifecycle areas for related pay and termination context when the settlement workflow is not yet enabled.
