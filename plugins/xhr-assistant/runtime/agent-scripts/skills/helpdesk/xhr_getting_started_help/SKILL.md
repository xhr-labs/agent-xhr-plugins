### Direct answer
### 👩💼 For HR Managers and Admins

**Onboard employees**  
Go to the [People]({{people_url}}) directory and click **Add Employee** to add new hires using a single form.  
The employee table shows names, employee IDs, departments, and roles. You can update details anytime from each employee’s profile.

**Manage documents**  
Use [Documents]({{documents_url}}) to upload company policies, handbooks, contracts, and other materials.  
Documents are stored centrally with categories and visibility controls. The Smart Agent can reference these documents when employees ask questions.

**Set roles and permissions**  
Navigate to [Workspace]({{workspace_url}}) → Company Account → Access & Permissions to define your organizational structure and assign roles.  
This controls who can view, edit, and manage information across the platform.

**Configure time-off policies**  
In [Time Off → Configuration]({{timeoff_configuration_url}}), define leave types, balances, and public holiday calendars.  
Employees can see their balances and holidays in **My Time Off**, while HR can review and approve requests in **Team Requests**.

**Create custom fields**  
Use [Custom Fields]({{custom_fields_url}}) (in Workspace settings) to add additional data points to employee profiles, allowing you to capture information unique to your business.

**Create forms and onboarding flows**
Use [Forms]({{forms_url}}) to build internal, public, onboarding, and review forms. Published forms support submissions, secure public links, and workflow assignment.

**Automate HR processes**
Use [Workflows]({{workflows_url}}) for event-based and date-based automation. Assigned work appears in Task Hub, while approval requests appear in Approval Hub.

**Define work locations**  
Set up multiple office or remote locations under [Work Locations]({{work_locations_url}}), so employees can be linked to the correct site.

**Review organization and workforce data**
Use the People Org Chart to inspect or update reporting relationships when permitted. Admin and HR Manager users can use People Reports for headcount, hiring, and offboarding trends.

**Track tasks and projects**  
Install the **Workbench** app from the [App Store]({{app_store_url}}) to enable **My Tasks** and **Spaces** for team collaboration and task tracking.

**Manage subscriptions and explore add-ons**  
In [Workspace → Explore Plans]({{explore_plans_url}}), view your current subscription and available upgrades.  
The [App Store]({{app_store_url}}) (currently in preview) also highlights upcoming add-ons such as **Virtual Data Room**, **Finance Hub**, and **Workbench**.

### 🙋 For Employees

**View and update your profile**  
Open [My Profile]({{my_profile_url}}) to see your personal details, role, compensation information, and attached documents.

**Request and track time off**  
In [Time Off]({{timeoff_url}}), view your leave balance and public holidays, then click **Request Time Off** to submit a request.  
Approved requests automatically appear on your calendar.

**Collaborate on tasks**  
If [Workbench]({{workbench_url}}) is installed, use **My Tasks** to track assignments and **Spaces** to collaborate with teammates on projects.

**Find information about colleagues**  
Use the [People]({{people_url}}) directory to view colleagues’ names, departments, and positions (subject to permissions).

**Ask the Smart Agent**  
Open the Lumi sidebar on desktop or the mobile chat popover to ask questions about policies, processes, or next steps. Lumi can preserve product context and reopen previous conversations.
The Smart Agent uses your company documents and live platform data to guide you—for example, helping you request leave or find internal information.
