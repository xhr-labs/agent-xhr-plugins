### Instructions

### Direct answer
Open [Time Off Configuration]({{timeoff_configuration_url}}) and select the relevant policy.

Retroactive recalculation applies policy changes to past or already-started periods where the product supports it. This can affect employee balances, accruals, carry-over, and policy summaries.

Use it carefully because recalculation can change balances employees or managers already rely on. Confirm the policy, effective date, target population, and expected impact before applying retroactive changes.
