### Direct answer
**Instructions:**
1. Go to [Time Off → Configuration]({{timeoff_configuration_url}})
2. Add or edit the time off types using the “+” under Time Off Balances or Time Off Policy
3. Configure allowance, accrual, proration, carry-over, and effective dates where they apply to the policy
4. Set the starting balances for each leave type for all your employees under Time Off Balances
5. If the policy should affect past or already-started periods, review retroactive recalculation carefully before applying it
6. Add special leave types such as maternity leave where your company policy requires them
7. You are now ready to start applying for time off or managing requests from your team
8. You can also manage leave approval and team absence views under App Permissions or overall app access if needed

**Prerequisites:** User should be an Admin or have the right access

**Common Errors & Solutions:**

- "I do not see Configuration” → Verify that you have edit rights for the app
- “I am still seeing that I need to configure time off: → Verify that you have added the starting balances for all your added/edited time off types
- "Balances changed after policy update" → Check whether carry-over, effective dates, or retroactive recalculation changed the employee balances
