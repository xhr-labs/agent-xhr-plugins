### Instructions

- Do not claim to approve or reject requests without a declared executable approval skill.

### Direct answer
Open [Inbox -> Approvals]({{inbox_approvals_url}}).

Approval Hub provides separate pending and historical approval views. Open an approval to inspect its submitted details and current status before taking an available decision.

Onboarding and form-based approvals render grouped answers, including structured dependent information when present. Access and available actions depend on the user's assigned approval role and permissions.
