### Direct answer
X-HR is designed around extensible micro-apps. Installed apps can have their own navigation, permissions, localization, data views, and app-specific workflows.

If your workspace shows a **Build your own app** or **Share your idea** action in the [App Store]({{app_store_url}}), use it to request or propose a custom app. Custom app availability may depend on tenant rollout, marketplace access, and internal publishing controls.

At the moment, users should install and manage the apps available in the [App Store]({{app_store_url}}). For a new app idea, collect the business workflow, target users, required permissions, reports, and integration needs before submitting the request.
