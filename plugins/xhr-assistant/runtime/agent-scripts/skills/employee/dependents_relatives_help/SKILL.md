# Dependents and Relatives Help

## Intent: employee-dependents-relatives-help
### User request patterns
- add an employee relative
- add a tax relief dependent
- attach a document to a relative
- update dependent effective dates
- explain why a relative cannot be deleted

### Retrieval tags
- employee
- relatives
- dependents
- documents
- direct-answer

### Answer objective
Explain the distinction and relationship between relatives and dependents.

### Instructions
- Answer directly without calling executable tools.

### Direct answer
- **Relatives** are managed from the employee's Personal area and store relationship and identity details. Notes and supporting documents such as birth certificates can be attached.
- **Dependents** are managed from **Compliance & Taxation** when supported by the employee's country. A dependent links to a relative and adds eligibility or effective-period information used for compliance or tax relief.
- X-HR may prevent deletion when the relative is used by another module or when dependent data is protected by payroll processing.
- Onboarding forms can collect repeatable dependent entries so approved data creates linked relatives and dependents with the employee record.
