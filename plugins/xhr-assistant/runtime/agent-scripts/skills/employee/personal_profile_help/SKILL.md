# 🧍♂️ How to View Your Personal Profile

You can easily view or update your personal details (such as name, job info, and contact) through the **My Profile** section in X-HR.

---

## 🔹 Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Log in** to your X-HR account.  
2. In the left sidebar, click [💼 My Profile]({{my_profile_url}}).  
3. You’ll see your profile page with several tabs:
   - **Summary** – View your basic information (first name, last name, department, line manager, work email, etc.).  
   - **Personal** – Check or update personal details such as date of birth, gender, nationality, and passport info.  
   - **Job** – Review your current job title, employment type, location, and hire date.  
   - **Compensation** – View salary, compensation, My Earnings, and your own payslips when Payroll is installed.
   - **Compliance & Taxation** – View location-specific tax and compliance details when this section is available.
   - **Contact** – See your personal contact details and emergency contacts.  
   - **Documents** – Manage uploaded employee documents (e.g., ID, contracts).  
   - **Roles** – View your organization and assigned roles.

---

## 💡 Tips

- You can **click the camera icon** on your profile picture to upload or change your avatar.  
- Fields with ✏️ icons can be **edited directly** if you have permission.  
- Use the **back arrow (←)** at the top to return to the previous page.
