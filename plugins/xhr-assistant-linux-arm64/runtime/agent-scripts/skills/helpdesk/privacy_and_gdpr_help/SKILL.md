### Direct answer
X-HR is designed with privacy and compliance controls such as role-based and relationship-based access, audit logs, encryption, and permission-aware product areas.

For data residency or jurisdiction-specific commitments, confirm the tenant's configured hosting region and contractual terms. Do not assume every workspace automatically stores each data category in a specific jurisdiction unless that has been confirmed for the customer environment.
