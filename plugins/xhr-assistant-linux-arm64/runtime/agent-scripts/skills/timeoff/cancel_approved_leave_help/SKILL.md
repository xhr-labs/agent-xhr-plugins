### Direct answer
## What happens if I cancel an approved leave request?

Open [Time Off]({{timeoff_url}}) to review the request.

Your leave balance will be restored based on the total days of the canceled request.

You can cancel an approved leave request only before it starts. If the leave starts today or has already passed in your timezone, you won’t be able to cancel it through this flow.
