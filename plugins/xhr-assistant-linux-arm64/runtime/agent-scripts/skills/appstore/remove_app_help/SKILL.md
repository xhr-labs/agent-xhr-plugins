### Direct answer
For apps that are already installed (such as **Time-off** or **Attendance Tracking**), you’ll see an **Uninstall** button in the [App Store]({{app_store_url}}).

Clicking **Uninstall** will remove the app from your workspace.  
Your core system data remains safe and intact, even if an app is removed.
