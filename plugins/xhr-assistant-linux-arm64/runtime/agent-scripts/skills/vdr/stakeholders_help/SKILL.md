### Answer objective

### Instructions
- Answer directly without calling executable tools.

### Direct answer
Open [VDR Stakeholders]({{vdr_stakeholders_url}}).

VDR stakeholder management keeps the people and groups that may participate in data-room sharing. Users can manage individual stakeholders, stakeholder groups, and investor-style contacts where permitted.

Stakeholder records support sharing and access workflows. Adding a stakeholder does not automatically grant access to every data room; access is controlled through data-room sharing and request decisions.
