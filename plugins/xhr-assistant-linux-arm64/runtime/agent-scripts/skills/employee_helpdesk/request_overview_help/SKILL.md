### Direct answer
Employee Helpdesk gives employees one place to submit HR questions and service requests. HR owns the case, communicates through the request conversation, adds internal notes or files, tracks assignment and SLA, and resolves or closes the request.

Typical request types include salary or employment certificates, visa and immigration support, payroll questions, benefits requests, personal-information changes, workplace issues, and general HR requests.
