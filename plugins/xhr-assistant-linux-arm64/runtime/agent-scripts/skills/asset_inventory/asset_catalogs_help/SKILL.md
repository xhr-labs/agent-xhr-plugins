### Direct answer
Open [Asset Categories]({{asset_categories_url}}) or [Vendors]({{asset_vendors_url}}).

Asset Inventory uses catalogs such as **Vendors** and **Categories** to classify assets consistently. Admins can use catalog pickers when creating or editing assets so asset records stay easier to filter, report, and reconcile.

Vendor and category management is separate from employee asset requests. Catalogs define the inventory vocabulary; request and lifecycle flows decide who receives an asset and what happens to it.
