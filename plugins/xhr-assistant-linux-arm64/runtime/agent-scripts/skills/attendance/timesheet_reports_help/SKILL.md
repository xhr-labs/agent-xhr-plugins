### Direct answer
Open [Attendance Reports]({{attendance_reports_url}}).

Attendance timesheet reports help managers and HR review attendance activity over time. Reports can include timesheet lists, detail views, annual report tables, progress hours, signed durations, and overtime-related values where configured.

Use reports to review attendance trends and exceptions. Use approval flows when the user needs to approve or reject specific submitted timesheets.
