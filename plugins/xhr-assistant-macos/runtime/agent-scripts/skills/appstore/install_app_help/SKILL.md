### Instructions
- Answer directly in text using the guidance below.

### Direct answer
1. Open the [App Store]({{app_store_url}}).
2. In the **Work with X-HR** section, find the app you want to install.
3. Click **Install** next to the app.

Once installed, the app will be available immediately in your sidebar or the relevant sections of the platform.
